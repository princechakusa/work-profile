## Captions

### reel-01-five-star
A 5-star stay is decided before the guest arrives.

Most of what makes a stay feel effortless happens out of sight: a clear arrival guide, access that works first time, an inspection after every clean, one reply standard the whole team holds to, and reviews treated as data.

Guests rarely notice any of it. They notice when it's missing.

What's the one behind-the-scenes step you would never skip?

#GuestExperience #HospitalityOperations #ShortTermRentals

### reel-02-ai-guests
AI can answer a guest in seconds. Should it?

My view: let AI draft, and keep people on the decisions. Start with the questions you answer fifty times a week, keep complaints and refunds with a human, and measure whether it is really helping.

How is your team using AI with customers right now?

#AI #CustomerExperience #Hospitality

### reel-03-sop
An SOP nobody follows is a wish list.

Write it with the people who do the job. Make the right way the easy way. Check it on the floor, not on paper. And when something doesn't fit, escalate it instead of working around it.

Which process on your team lives only on paper?

#Operations #ProcessImprovement #Leadership

### reel-04-reviews
Feedback is free consulting, if you read it properly.

Tag every complaint. Count instead of remembering. Fix the top three. Tell the team what changed, then watch next month's reviews.

How does your team turn feedback into fixes?

#CustomerExperience #GuestExperience #DataDriven

### reel-05-leading
Teams don't follow charts. They follow clarity.

When concierge, maintenance and housekeeping work as one, it comes down to a shared morning brief, one owner per issue, an escalation path everyone knows, and credit for the problems that never reached the guest.

What keeps your cross-functional team in sync?

#Leadership #TeamManagement #Operations

### reel-06-trust
Due diligence isn't just for banks.

In short-term rentals, trust comes from quiet checks: knowing who is staying, making sure the booking adds up, escalating what doesn't fit, and keeping the record.

What's one check your business does that customers never see?

#RiskManagement #Compliance #Hospitality
